Who we are

We are a private culinary event format. Not a restaurant. Not a venue. An experience that arrives, creates something extraordinary, and moves on.

One table. A hand-selected location. A chef who has worked in Michelin-starred kitchens. A tasting menu built on food culture, personal history, and storytelling.

We draw from the tradition of the speakeasy — discovered, not advertised. Found, not searched for. The kind of evening you talk about for years.

The concept

A clandestine
culinary movement

Private dining concepts share their philosophy with the speakeasy clubs born during Prohibition — exclusive, discovered rather than advertised, experienced rather than consumed.

JoEs TaBLe operates at the forefront of a new culinary movement, alongside NOMA's pop-up concept, Bruno's philosophy at Verjus TABLE, and Junghyun's ATOMIX. An event format rooted in the conviction that a table is a stage, and what happens around it is culture.

Reservations & policies
How to book
All events are by reservation only. Submit an enquiry via our booking form and we will confirm availability and send payment instructions. No event is confirmed until the booking fee is received.
Cancellation policy
Grace period (up to 48 hours before the event): If you cancel within this window, your booking fee will be fully released. Group and corporate events require minimum 7 days notice.

No-show: The full reservation amount will be charged. Non-refundable.
Dietary requirements
If you have allergies, intolerances, or dietary restrictions, please contact us in advance at support@josestable.com. We will do our best to accommodate, but menu adjustments on the day cannot be guaranteed.
