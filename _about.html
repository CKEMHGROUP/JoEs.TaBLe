Who we are

We are an exclusive private culinary event format, producing one-table experiences at hand-selected locations. Each event is designed, staffed, and executed from scratch — for the occasion, for the guests, for the brief.

We are not a restaurant. We hold no premises. We have no fixed address to visit. What we have is a network of outstanding chefs, venue partners, and logistics operators who assemble wherever we are needed, and a culinary vision built on food culture as the deepest form of human connection.

We serve corporate clients who want something genuinely different, and private guests who understand that the most memorable evenings are never the obvious ones.

The concept

Private dining concepts share their philosophy and nature with the speakeasy bars and clubs born during Prohibition in Chicago. Although those were born out of political circumstances, the core sense is the same — something exclusive, discovered rather than advertised, experienced rather than consumed. The most famous example of its time was Club 21 in New York City.

JoEs TaBLe operates at the forefront of a new culinary movement — alongside NOMA's innovative pop-up concept, Milesi's UNA, Bruno's philosophy at Verjus TABLE, and Junghyun's ATOMIX. An event format rooted in the same conviction: that a table is a stage, and what happens around it is culture.

We operate on a reservation-only basis. All events are by enquiry. A seasonal, multi-course tasting menu is prepared for each occasion, with wine pairings selected in person by our sommelier from producers who share our philosophy.

The event format
One table. Curated location. Changing throughout the year. A multi-course sensory occasion where each dish carries a story.
Our philosophy
We are not focused on molecular tricks or theatrical effects. We approach your taste with a different perspective of what food should be: memories, stories, and honest produce.

Reservations & policies
How to book
All events are by reservation only. Submit an enquiry via our booking form and we will confirm availability and send payment instructions. No event is confirmed until the booking fee is received.
Cancellation policy
Grace period (up to 48 hours before the event): If you cancel within this window, your booking fee will be fully released. Grace periods for group and corporate events vary — minimum 7 days before the event date.

No-show: If you do not cancel before the grace period ends, the full reservation amount will be charged. No-shows are non-refundable.

Cancellation with notice: No charge within the grace period (5% processing fee applies).
Transferable bookings
If you are unable to attend after the grace period ends, you may transfer your booking to someone else. We ask all guests to notify us as early as possible.
Dietary requirements
If you have allergies, intolerances, or dietary restrictions, please contact us in advance. We will do our best to accommodate, but menu adjustments on the day are not guaranteed.
For full terms, policies, or any enquiries: support@josestable.com
In the press

Finding Joe's pop-up spot in a dark alley, far from our hotel, in a neighbourhood where our taxi driver warned us to keep our hands on our wallets, heightened our sense of adventure. Soon, we were spotted by one of Joe's staff and invited in.

Like a great Broadway show, the meal was divided into acts: The Overture, The Prelude, The Suite, and The Finale. During The Overture, we were treated to Cicchetti Iberico — three different and fabulous strips of ham on crusty bread.

The wine continued to flow, and the food was amazing. The most touching story of the night came from Joe, who previously worked at some of the finest restaurants, honing his craft as a chef. But his true love and passion for cooking came from his late grandmother, whom he loved dearly. In creating Joe's Table, he paid homage to her. His eyes welled up as he told us. Our eyes welled, too.

What I learned is that it is not about the eating. It is all about the sharing.

Richard Reiss
