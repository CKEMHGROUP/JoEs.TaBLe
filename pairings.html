How to add a pairing
01
Choose your pairing when booking
We recommend choosing your pairing when submitting your reservation enquiry. This allows us to reserve the wines and ensure they are prepared correctly for your evening.
02
Why booking in advance matters
Some wines need aeration to reach their best characteristics. Time is part of the experience. Booking in advance allows us to prepare your pairing with care.
03
Or decide at the event
You may also choose your pairing on the evening. Our sommelier or chef will be happy to assist and advise. All pairings are per person.
