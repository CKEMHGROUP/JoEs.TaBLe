How to add a pairing
01
Choose when you book

We recommend choosing your pairing when submitting your reservation. This allows us to reserve the wines and prepare them correctly for your evening.

02
Why booking in advance matters

Some wines need aeration to reach their best characteristics. Time is part of the experience. Advance booking allows us to prepare your pairing with the care it deserves.

03
Or decide on the evening

You may also choose your pairing on the evening. Our sommelier or chef will be happy to advise. All pairings are per person.
