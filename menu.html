The concept

A clandestine
culinary movement

We design and produce exclusive private culinary events at hand-selected locations. Each event is a one-table experience — part private gathering, part cultural occasion — for clients who value discretion, quality, and a truly considered evening.

We are not a restaurant. We are an event format: a curated culinary concept that arrives, creates something extraordinary, and moves on. Every location is chosen. Every team is assembled for the occasion.

What we do

The full event experience

From first enquiry to the final course, every element is designed, sourced, and managed by JoEs TaBLe.

01

Venue curation

Hand-selected locations matched to your group, occasion, and desired atmosphere. Private residences, galleries, boutique hotels, and emblematic spaces.

02

Culinary design

A seasonal, multi-course tasting menu built around culinary history and Joe's personal journey through Michelin-starred kitchens. Each dish carries a story.

03

Wine programme

Hand-selected pairings from low-intervention producers. Four pairing tiers across Portugal, Spain, and France. Sourced in person by our sommelier.

04

Team & logistics

A curated team of chefs, service staff, and logistics professionals assembled for each event. Full setup and breakdown managed by JoEs TaBLe.

05

Corporate events

Custom-designed events for company teams, client entertainment, and executive occasions. Groups from 6 to 50+. Tailored to your brief and guests.

06

By reservation only

All events by reservation. Private clients enquire via our form. Corporate clients contact us directly for a tailored event proposal.

Corporate & private events

Designed for

We design and produce bespoke private culinary events for corporate teams, client entertainment, executive occasions, and private gatherings. Each event is built from scratch around your brief, your guests, and your goals.
